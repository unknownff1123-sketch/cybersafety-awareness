3 PROBLEMS WE ADDRESS

Three everyday problems that put us at risk.

These are the problems our awareness sessions focus on, with how each one happens and what you can do about it.

Problem 1

Lack of Awareness About Online Fraud

Many people lose money because they do not know how online scams work. Fraudsters rely on this, and they use fear, urgency and greed to make us act before we think.

How it happens

  • A call or SMS says your bank KYC has expired and your account will be blocked.
  • A message says you won a lottery, prize or cashback and must pay a small fee to claim it.
  • A fake job offer asks for a registration fee.
  • A fake customer care number found on a search page asks you to install a remote-access app.

How to stay safe

  • Learn the common scams. If an offer sounds too good to be true, it is.
  • Never act on pressure. Real banks do not threaten you to act in minutes.
  • Check with the official app, website or number printed on your card.
  • Tell your family and friends about scams you hear of.

Problem 2

Unsafe Sharing of Personal Information

Every detail we share can be used against us. OTPs, passwords, card details, Aadhaar and PAN copies, and even a public birthday or phone number help fraudsters take over accounts or pretend to be us.

How it happens

  • A caller pretending to be bank staff asks for your OTP, PIN or CVV.
  • Photos of tickets, cards or ID documents are posted on social media.
  • Public profiles show your phone number, birthday, school and location.
  • Personal details are typed into unknown websites or forms.

How to stay safe

  • Never share OTPs, passwords or bank details with anyone. Bank staff never ask for them.
  • Keep your social media profiles private and limit who can see your details.
  • Do not post photos of documents, cards or tickets.
  • Share personal details only on official apps and websites.

Problem 3

Fake Social Media Accounts & Phishing Links

Anyone can create a fake profile in minutes, using a real person’s photo and name. Phishing links copy the look of real banks, shops and apps to steal your login details and money.

How it happens

  • A new profile copying your friend asks for urgent money.
  • Strangers send friend requests, then share a link to a "great offer" or "free gift".
  • An SMS or WhatsApp link opens a page that looks like your bank, but the web address is slightly different.
  • Fake brand pages promise giveaways or discounts.

How to stay safe

  • Verify before you trust. Call the friend on their usual number before sending money.
  • Do not accept requests from strangers or click unknown links.
  • Look at the full web address before you open a link or enter a password.
  • Report and block fake accounts, and use two-step login (2FA).

COMMON THREATS

Know the danger before it happens.

Cybercriminals use different techniques to trick users. Understanding common scams is the first step toward protection.

Financial Fraud
