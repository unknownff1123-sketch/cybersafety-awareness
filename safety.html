CYBER SAFETY

Protect Yourself Online

Simple habits can significantly reduce your exposure to common online scams.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts. Consider using a trusted password manager.

🔢

Protect Your OTP

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with another person, even if they claim to be from a bank.

🔗

Check Links

Before clicking a link, check the website address carefully and avoid unexpected links.

📲

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication wherever available to add another layer of account protection.

🔄

Update Devices

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated.

👀

Think Before You Click

Be cautious when a message creates urgency, fear or promises unexpected rewards.

✅ Do

  • Verify suspicious messages.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Use official websites and applications.

❌ Don't

  • Share OTP or PIN.
  • Click unknown links.
  • Install unknown applications.
  • Send money to unknown people.
  • Share passwords with anyone.
