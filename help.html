STEP BY STEP

What to do right now

1

Call 1930 immediately

Tell them the amount, time, and the account or UPI ID the money went to.

2

Call your bank

Use the number on your card or the official app. Ask to block card, UPI and net banking.

3

Secure your accounts

Change passwords from a safe device, log out everywhere, and uninstall any app the fraudster told you to install.

4

Save the evidence

Screenshots, chats, phone numbers, transaction IDs, links and bank SMS. Do not delete anything.

5

File a complaint

Register at cybercrime.gov.in, or visit your nearest police station to file an FIR.

Keep ready

  • Transaction ID / UTR number
  • Date, time and amount
  • Scammer's phone number or profile link
  • Screenshots of chats and links
  • Your bank statement

Never do

  • Don't pay more money to "get it back"
  • Don't trust anyone who offers to recover your money for a fee
  • Don't delete chats or call history
  • Don't feel ashamed. Report early.
